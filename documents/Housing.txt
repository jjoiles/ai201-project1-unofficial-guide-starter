View Residence Halls
Howard University has nine Residence Halls on campus, and three University sponsored Residence Halls off campus in private apartment complexes. Each residence hall has 24/7 controlled entry, laundry facilities, and other amenities. The halls are managed by Campus Apartments and other off-campus management partners with additional facilities support from REDCAM.
