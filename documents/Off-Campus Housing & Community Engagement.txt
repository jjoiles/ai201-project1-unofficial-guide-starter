Off-Campus Housing & Community Engagement
The Office of Off-Campus Housing and Community Engagement supports students who live off-campus and provides community engagement services. OCHCE offers resources to help students find off-campus and provides guidance about moving in, signing leases, and being responsible members of their new communities.

OCHCE also provides students with volunteer opportunities and manages a food pantry available to all Howard University students.
