Title: Title: Find Off-Campus Housing
Source: https://studentaffairs.howard.edu/housing/find-off-campus-housing

The Howard experience extends beyond campus.

College is full of "firsts," including finding your first apartment. Living off campus can help you build independence while staying connected to Howard.

Students should distinguish between two housing paths:

Independent off-campus housing: Properties listed through Howard's off-campus housing portal are independently owned and managed. Students apply to and enter a lease directly with the property owner or manager.

University-sponsored housing: These locations are identified on the Explore Residence Halls pages and follow Howard's housing assignment process.

The Office of Residence Life can provide general resources for evaluating off-campus options and being a respectful member of the surrounding community.
